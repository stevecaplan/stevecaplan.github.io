New novel · Spring 2027 · Soncata Press

October’s Ashes

One family. Two children. A war that pulls them apart.

In the aftermath of October 7, neuroscientist Yariv Brickman watches his world unravel: his son enlists in the Israeli army, his daughter joins a radical pro-Palestinian movement, and his wife embraces an increasingly uncompromising nationalism. In a New York living in the shadow of war, Yariv struggles to hold together a family being torn apart by forces far beyond his control.

The scientist behind the stories

Steve Caplan, Ph.D., is Professor and Vice Chair of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at the University of Nebraska Medical Center. His research is funded by the National Institute of General Medical Sciences, and he serves on NIH grant review panels. He is deeply committed to advancing fundamental scientific research and to mentoring and training the next generation of scientists.

The author of Today’s Curiosity Is Tomorrow’s Cure, a nonfiction book exploring the importance of fundamental scientific research, Caplan is also a novelist. A U.S.-born Canadian and Israeli, he draws on his cross-cultural experiences, military service, and scientific career to create psychologically complex, deeply human characters grappling with trauma, moral conscience, identity, and mental health. His fiction explores the intersection of personal and political conflict, the fragility of human relationships, and the often-uncomfortable tension between our convictions and our actions.
